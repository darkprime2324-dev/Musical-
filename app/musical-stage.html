Lyrics

Lyrics for the current song. Songs you add get their lyrics from LRCLIB, a free open lyrics database, and are then auto-synced to your recording. Lines with timestamps like [01:23.50] sync exactly, and <01:23.90> tags inside a line time single words, which places every word-star on its exact beat. Plain lines are spread evenly across the song, or use Sync by tapping to time them yourself while the song plays.

While a song plays, [ and ] shift all lyrics 0.1 s earlier or later, { and } by a full second. The shift is saved as an [offset:] line.

Songs play in order and move to the next one automatically. Open a whole folder at once: a .lrc file with the same name as a song is used as its lyrics, and songs without one look theirs up on LRCLIB. You can also drop files onto the sky.
